BEFORE YOU APPLY

What you may need

Having the right information available will make the application process easier and faster.

  • Student Information

    Basic personal and contact details of the student.

  • Parent or Guardian Information

    Contact information and relationship to the student.

  • Previous School Information

    Details about the student's previous or current school.

  • Academic Records

    Recent academic information where applicable.

  • Supporting Documents

    Any additional documents requested during the admission process.
